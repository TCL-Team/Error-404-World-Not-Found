```markdown
**ERROR 404: World Not Found** is a horror mod about a world that is being deleted while you stand
in it — and the thing that is doing the deleting.

## What it actually does

- **The world loses integrity.** 100% → 0% across five phases. Chunks are deleted for real: they come
  apart block by block, in front of you, with the sound of a save file failing.
- **Your own chunk can go.** From phase 3, after your first death, the mod can pick the chunk you are
  standing in: nine seconds of warning, a countdown on the action bar, a heartbeat that speeds up.
  Sprint out and you watch it collapse behind you. Stay and you are deleted with it.
- **422 is in the file already.** It never "spawns". It replays your own route from a minute ago,
  aims at where you will be in two seconds, learns whether you hide underground or stand still, and
  breaks whatever is between it and you. It cannot be outrun forever and it cannot be killed — only
  interrupted.
- **Anything can happen.** From phase 3 the world fires one named event every one to three minutes:
  a chunk collapsing, your hotbar reshuffling, lightning with no storm, every door in twenty blocks
  opening, a full-volume sound directly behind you, something spawning behind you already angry,
  or being displaced eighteen blocks sideways.
- **The title screen is not yours anymore.** A deleted-world panorama, an original `ERROR 404` wordmark,
  scanlines, drifting error logs, and a rare burst of static. Shipped as a switchable resource pack.
- **Twelve structures that should not exist**, from a staircase to nowhere to a sealed obsidian vault
  with every floor plate wired to nothing and a heartbeat inside.

## It is reversible

Every deleted block is journaled. `/error404 restore` puts the world back exactly as it was. The mod
also zips the region folder before a session touches anything (on by default).

## Difficulty

`chill` (it watches, never touches) · `standard` (it hurts, the rules are learnable) · `nightmare`
(it is faster than you, it eats walls, it wakes you up). Switchable live.

Settings are in-game: **Mod Menu → ERROR 404 → settings**, or `/error404config` if you do not use
Mod Menu. Every destructive switch has a tooltip that says exactly what it destroys.

## Warning

Loud sounds, sudden flashes, fake crash screens, and world damage that feels permanent even when it
is not. There is a photosensitivity mode in the settings. Do not install this on a server where the
players did not agree to a horror mod.

## Requirements

Minecraft **1.21.11**, **Fabric Loader 0.20.1+**, **Fabric API**. Mod Menu is optional.
```
